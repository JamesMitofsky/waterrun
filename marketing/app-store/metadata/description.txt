Half the drinking fountains on a map are broken, bone dry, or long gone. Water Run fixes that the only way it can be fixed: someone goes and looks. That someone is you, on your run.

FIND WATER THAT WORKS
The Nearby map colours every public fountain by how recently a person actually checked it: green for this season, orange for months ago, red for a year or more. You know what you're running toward before you get there.

PLAN A LOOP PAST THE UNCHECKED ONES
Tap the fountains you want to pass, or touch and hold the map to route through a spot. Water Run draws a real running route through them, as a loop or one way, and tells you how far it is. Reverse it with one tap.

RUN IT. WE'LL POINT THE WAY.
On the run you get what matters at a glance: distance and direction to the next fountain, the next turn, and when it was last checked. Arrive, and the survey opens on its own.

ONE TAP, MID-STRIDE
Working? Tap once and keep going. Problem? Two more taps say whether it's partially working, totally out of order, or not there at all. Add details when you want to: bottle filler, dog bowl, seasonal, a short note.

ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
Your next stop, distance and turn stay on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, so your phone can stay in your pocket.

FOUND ONE THAT ISN'T ON THE MAP?
Touch and hold where it stands, or tap + where you're standing, and add it.

IT GOES ON THE PUBLIC MAP
Every check is saved to OpenStreetMap, the free map that countless other apps and services draw on. So a fountain you confirm today helps the next thirsty runner, cyclist, parent or dog walker, whatever app they use. Start checking straight away; your updates wait safely on your phone until you sign in with a free OpenStreetMap account.

Free. No ads. Made by runners, for everyone who gets thirsty.
